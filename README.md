# Vendor-Performance-Dashboard-
“Built a sales analysis dashboard using Excel, Power BI, and Tableau to analyze vendor performance, sales trends, and inventory. Generated insights to improve pricing, reduce unsold inventory, and support business decision-making.”

## 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing vendor performance using retail sales and purchase data. The goal is to evaluate how different vendors contribute to overall business performance and identify opportunities to improve profitability, reduce risks, and optimize supplier relationships.

An interactive dashboard was developed to provide clear insights into vendor contribution, sales trends, profit margins, and inventory efficiency.

## 🎯 Objective
Analyze vendor contribution to sales and purchases
Identify high-performing and low-performing vendors
Evaluate profitability across vendors
Support better vendor management and decision-making

## ❓ Problem Statement
Businesses often depend heavily on a small number of vendors and struggle with:

Uneven vendor performance
Low sales from certain vendors
High inventory costs and unsold stock

This project aims to identify these issues and provide insights to improve vendor performance and business efficiency.

## 📂 Dataset Overview
Type: Retail Sales & Vendor Data
Description: Transaction-level dataset containing vendor, sales, and purchase details

Key Fields:

Vendor Name
Purchase Quantity
Sales Quantity
Total Sales
Total Purchases
Gross Profit
Profit Margin
Stock Turnover

## ⚙️ Methodology
Data Cleaning
Removed invalid and inconsistent records
Filtered out zero sales and negative profit transactions
Exploratory Data Analysis
Analyzed vendor-wise sales, purchases, and profitability
Identified trends and outliers
Vendor Segmentation
Classified vendors into:
High-performing vendors
Low-performing vendors
Dashboard Development
Built interactive dashboards to visualize:
Vendor contribution
Sales vs profit
Inventory performance

## 📈 Key Insights
Top vendors contribute a major share of total purchases, indicating high dependency risk
Some vendors generate high profit margins but low sales volume
Presence of unsold inventory impacts overall efficiency
Bulk purchasing reduces unit cost and improves profitability

## 🔍 Key Findings
1. Vendor Contribution Imbalance

A small group of vendors dominates total sales and purchases, creating potential supply chain risks.

2. Low-Performing Vendors

Some vendors show low sales despite higher profit margins, indicating opportunities for pricing or marketing improvements.

3. Inventory Inefficiency

Slow-moving inventory tied to certain vendors leads to increased storage cost and reduced cash flow.

4. Profitability Variation

Different vendors follow different profitability patterns, requiring tailored strategies.
